# Code of Conduct

BoxBox should be a friendly place for people building and running self-hosted software.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent, especially with new contributors and self-hosters asking for help.
- Keep criticism focused on the code, docs, behavior, or deployment details.
- Share enough context to help others reproduce problems.
- Respect privacy: do not post someone else's secrets, hostnames, private paths, logs, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive or hostile responses to good-faith questions.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic posting.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Pushing people toward unsafe deployment practices without clear warnings.

## Reporting

If something in issues, discussions, pull requests, or other project spaces violates this code, contact the maintainer through GitHub or open a private security advisory when sensitive information is involved.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may block participants when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
