## Missing or partial, ranked

1. **GIO device and mount coverage** — GPUI sees UDisks2 block volumes, while a Linux file manager also needs GVfs remote, archive, MTP, and credential-driven mounts, plus consistent pre-unmount/eject handling. (Rows 44–52; device and encrypted-volume breadth is a known gap in UI_MAP.md.)
2. **Folder-target drag-and-drop** — GPUI imports drops on the pane only; users need folder-row/tile destinations and the expected copy/move/link/execute choices. (Rows 62–64; known gap.)
3. **Details-view columns and tree navigation** — Four static columns omit configurable metadata, saved widths, and expandable directory rows with Left/Right navigation. (Rows 36, 39–42.)
4. **Recursive folder summaries** — GPUI reports zero for folder sizes and cannot provide recursive file/folder counts, allocated size, progress, or cancellation. (Rows 26–29; multi-selection summary is a known gap.)
5. **Destination conflict choices** — Skip and Keep both work, but replace, merge, and rename are missing; these choices matter during routine copy/move operations. (Row 55.)
6. **Keyboard and selection parity** — Home/End/Page navigation, row type-ahead, keyboard context menus, rubber-band selection, and invert selection are missing or partial. (Rows 30, 34–38; Home/End/Page and type-ahead are known gaps.)
7. **Standard FileManager1 behavior** — ShowItems does not select the item and ShowItemProperties does not open properties, both breaking common desktop integrations. (Rows 24–25; ShowItems selection is a known gap.)
8. **Executable and launcher safety prompts** — Thunar asks whether to execute/open scripts and whether to trust desktop launchers; GPUI has no matching flow. (Rows 58–59.)
9. **Assistive-technology semantics** — The Thunar directory listing exposes a named directory-pane role; no equivalent accessibility role is assigned in the GPUI view. (Row 43.)
10. **Legacy Thunar DBus methods** — The app lacks Thunar's own DBus service for bulk rename, templates, link operations, execution, and other automation; lower priority than standard FileManager1 and interactive file-manager workflows. (Rows 1–22.)
11. **Launcher editing** — Desktop launchers cannot be edited from the file manager. (Row 70.)
