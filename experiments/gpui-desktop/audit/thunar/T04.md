## Missing or partial, ranked

1. **Configurable detailed-list columns** (rows 39–44): users cannot choose useful metadata, resize or reorder columns, restore their preferred layout, or show contained-item counts.
2. **Complete mount-on-open and GVfs support** (rows 1–7): GPUI handles UDisks device mounting, but not GIO shortcuts, general mountables, network/MTP filesystems or encrypted-volume workflows. `known`.
3. **Full application association management** (rows 20–25): the basic Open With flow exists, but custom executable selection, remove/forget, Properties-only default selection, and the “no default yet” behavior are missing or partial. The map’s Open With “Local ✗” entry is stale relative to this source tree.
4. **Clipboard interoperability and image paste** (rows 32–38): image paste is absent, the menu does not reflect an external clipboard, and GPUI does not clear a published cut clipboard after move.
5. **Context-menu customization and action coverage** (rows 48–51): users cannot hide/reorder actions or add their own actions and separators; the current menu has a smaller fixed catalog.
6. **Compact directory view** (rows 45–47): the density toggle only tightens list rows; it does not provide Thunar’s multi-column compact layout, middle truncation option, per-view zoom, or accessible role/name.
7. **Clipboard feedback and retention edge cases** (rows 36–38): external read errors are silent and the Wayland provider lacks an explicit persisted selection owner.
